## Bundled font

`assets/fonts/NotoSansKR-Subset.otf` is a Korean/Latin subset of Noto Sans CJK KR Regular, obtained from the installed Noto Sans CJK font collection. Noto is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full upstream attribution/license text shipped by the font package is retained at `assets/fonts/LICENSE-NOTO.txt`.

The subset includes printable ASCII, Korean Hangul syllables and compatibility jamo, and selected punctuation. No unrelated font glyphs are required from the user's operating system.
